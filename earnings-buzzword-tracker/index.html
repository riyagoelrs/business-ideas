HOW TO READ THIS

Signal, not sentiment.

SignalCall is designed to answer a simple question: which words are entering corporate vocabulary fastest?

  • Momentum = change in exact phrase mentions vs. the prior quarter.
  • Adoption = how many companies used the term in their latest analyzed call.
  • Heatmap = concentration of a narrative by company.
  • Pull transcripts = fetch the calls automatically, then count phrases locally in your browser.
  • Manual fallback = paste or upload transcript text only when you want a no-API workflow.
