How this works
Restaurant universe comes live from OpenStreetMap for the selected city. This is why search works even when a restaurant has no hype data yet.

Hype Score (0–100) uses free-source attention signals: TikTok velocity + public web/news velocity + optional reservation scarcity.

Quality Score is separate. Beli and public structured rating metadata are quality inputs, not hype.

Hype Gap = Hype − Quality. Positive can flag attention outrunning quality; negative can surface sleepers.

Coverage tells you how much of the free signal stack actually contributed. Missing sources are reweighted, never silently counted as zero.
