How the score works
Hype Score (0–100) = 65% TikTok + 25% public web/news + 10% reservation scarcity when available. Missing sources are reweighted, not counted as zero.

TikTok emphasizes 7-day mention growth, mentions, views and engagement.

Web uses recent article/website mention volume, 7-day velocity and source breadth from GDELT.

Quality Score = primarily Beli, plus structured rating metadata from the restaurant's own site when present.

Hype Gap = Hype − Quality. Positive can mean attention is outrunning quality; negative can surface sleepers.

Coverage = how much of the free source stack actually contributed. A score with broader coverage deserves more confidence.
