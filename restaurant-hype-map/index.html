How to read the scores
Beli rating (0–10) = the score published on Beli's public NYC category list. This is not your personal Beli rating.

Quality index (0–100) = Beli rating × 10. It is just an easier visual scale, not a separate data source.

Category percentile (0–100) = where the restaurant sits inside that Beli category list. 100 = #1 in the category; 0 = last on the list.

Pin color = quality tier. Pin size = category percentile, so higher-ranked spots look larger.

Hype score: intentionally not shown yet. The old version used a weak proxy. The next real hype layer should come from TikTok mention volume + velocity + engagement from your scraper.

Pins are placed at neighborhood level with slight offsets so the map works without a paid geocoding key. Click “Open in Google Maps” for the exact current listing.
